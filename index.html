01 / ABOUT

A CLINICAL PRACTICE WITH A SYSTEMS PERSPECTIVE

Care can be rigorous
and deeply human.

I’m a Licensed Clinical Social Worker and Council-Approved Supervisor based in Houston. I founded Mind X Associates to advance trauma-informed, evidence-based, and culturally responsive care.

My work spans psychotherapy, clinical supervision, professional consultation, education, and community building. I bring an intersectional lens to how culture, systems, identity, and lived experience shape mental health and the practice of care.
