01 / ABOUT

Building care that is
evidence-based and human.

I am a Licensed Clinical Social Worker and Council-Approved Supervisor based in Houston, Texas. I founded Mind X Associates, a psychotherapy collective focused on trauma-informed, evidence-based, and culturally responsive care.

My work spans clinical practice, supervision, professional consultation, and education. In clinical work, I support adults navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, burnout, and cross-cultural adaptation. My practice draws from CBT, ACT, DBT, EMDR, Motivational Interviewing, and IFS.

“My goal is to strengthen the field through collaboration — partnering with colleagues, building supervision relationships, and creating spaces for knowledge-sharing and growth.”
